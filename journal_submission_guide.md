# Journal Submission Guide

This document outlines the strategy and guidelines for submitting the BioSignal research to high-impact academic journals after the initial conference presentation.

## 1. Target Journals
Once the ICEE paper is published (which establishes our preliminary results), we will target the following journals:
- **IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Circuits and Systems (TBioCAS):** Focuses on the intersection of hardware and biomedical applications.
- **IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics (JBHI):** Focuses on AI, signal processing, and data management in healthcare.
- **Biomedical Signal Processing and Control (Elsevier):** A highly cited journal for practical biomedical engineering applications.

## 2. Journal Submission Checklist
- [ ] **Extended Manuscript:** The conference paper will be expanded by at least 40% with new data (e.g., real MIT-BIH validation).
- [ ] **Cover Letter:** A formal letter to the Editor-in-Chief explaining the novelty and significance of the work.
- [ ] **Author Declarations:** Disclosing that this work is original and not under consideration elsewhere.
- [ ] **Formatting:** Following the specific LaTeX/Word template of the target journal.
- [ ] **Graphical Abstract:** A visual summary of the project (we have already prepared `graphical_abstract.md`).

## 3. The Peer Review Process
1. **Submission:** Uploading the manuscript to the journal's online system.
2. **Initial Screening:** The editor checks if the paper fits the journal's scope.
3. **Peer Review:** 2-3 anonymous experts will review the paper and provide feedback (Major/Minor revisions).
4. **Rebuttal:** We will respond to the reviewers' comments and revise the manuscript accordingly.
5. **Acceptance:** Final approval for publication.

## 4. Open Access vs. Traditional
- We will likely choose the **Open Access** option (if funding allows) so that researchers and patients worldwide can read our work for free.
