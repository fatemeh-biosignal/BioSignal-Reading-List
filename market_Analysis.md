# Market Analysis & Target Audience

This document outlines the potential market and target customers for the wearable ECG device.

## 1. Target Market (بازار هدف)
- **Hospitals & Clinics:** For continuous monitoring of post-surgery patients or those with heart conditions.
- **Elderly Care Homes:** For round-the-clock monitoring of elderly residents.
- **Home Healthcare:** For patients who need remote monitoring from the comfort of their homes.
- **Sports & Fitness:** For athletes who want to monitor their heart health during intense training.

## 2. Value Proposition (ارزش پیشنهادی)
- **Cost-Effective:** Low-power design reduces battery replacement costs.
- **Privacy-Preserving:** On-device TinyML ensures patient data is not sent to the cloud.
- **Continuous Monitoring:** Unlike a 10-second ECG at a clinic, this device monitors the heart 24/7.

## 3. Competitors (رقبا)
- **Apple Watch / Samsung Galaxy Watch:** They offer ECG features, but they are expensive and not designed for continuous medical-grade monitoring.
- **Holter Monitors:** Used in hospitals, but they are bulky and not suitable for long-term daily wear.
- **Our Advantage:** A low-cost, lightweight patch that uses Compressive Sensing to extend battery life significantly.
