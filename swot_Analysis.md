# SWOT Analysis

This document provides a strategic analysis of the BioSignal project's Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats.

## 🟢 Strengths (نقاط قوت)
- **Low-Cost Hardware:** Estimated prototype cost of ~$33.
- **Privacy-Preserving AI:** On-device TinyML inference (no cloud data transmission).
- **Energy Efficient:** Compressive Sensing reduces data size by 70%.
- **Open-Source Foundation:** Transparent algorithms and code for the scientific community.

## 🔴 Weaknesses (نقاط ضعف)
- **No Physical Prototype Yet:** Currently relies on software simulation.
- **Synthetic Data:** The AI is trained on synthetic signals; real-world validation is pending.
- **Single-Lead ECG:** May miss certain arrhythmias that require a 12-lead ECG.

## 🔵 Opportunities (فرصت‌ها)
- **Growing Telemedicine Market:** High demand for remote patient monitoring post-COVID.
- **Government Support:** Iranian Ministry of Health's push for local MedTech innovation.
- **Aging Population:** Increasing need for continuous cardiac monitoring in elderly care.

## 🟠 Threats (تهدیدها)
- **Regulatory Delays:** The IRC (Food and Drug Administration of Iran) approval process can be slow.
- **Competition:** Tech giants (Apple, Samsung) and established medical device companies.
- **Sanctions & Supply Chain:** Potential difficulty in importing specific electronic components.
