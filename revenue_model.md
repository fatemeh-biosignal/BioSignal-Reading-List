# Revenue Model

This document outlines the financial strategy and revenue streams for the BioSignal project.

## 1. Hardware Sales (One-Time)
- **Product:** The reusable BioSignal Core Device and the disposable patch pack.
- **Pricing Strategy:** The core device is sold at a low margin (e.g., $25) to encourage adoption. The disposable patches are sold at a higher margin (e.g., $10 for a pack of 4).
- **Target:** Hospitals and individual patients.

## 2. Subscription Model (Recurring Revenue)
- **Product:** BioSignal Premium App.
- **Features:** Advanced AI analytics, doctor teleconsultation, and unlimited data history.
- **Pricing:** $5 per month.
- **Target:** High-risk patients and elderly care facilities.

## 3. Data Licensing (B2B)
- **Product:** Anonymized, aggregated cardiac data.
- **Target:** Pharmaceutical companies and AI research centers.
- **Pricing:** Negotiated contracts.
- **Note:** This is strictly compliant with patient privacy laws (GDPR/HIPAA).

## 4. Financial Projections (First Year)
- Assuming 1,000 users in the first year:
  - Hardware Sales: 1,000 × $25 = $25,000
  - Subscription: 500 users × $5 × 12 months = $30,000
  - **Total Year 1 Revenue:** ~$55,000
