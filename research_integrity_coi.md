# Research Integrity & Conflict of Interest (COI) Statement

This document outlines the ethical standards and transparency policies for the BioSignal project.

## 1. Conflict of Interest (COI) Declaration
- **Financial COI:** The founder (Fatemeh Karimi) declares no financial conflicts of interest. The project is currently self-funded and independent, with no influence from commercial entities.
- **Personal COI:** There are no personal or professional relationships that could inappropriately influence the design, execution, or reporting of this research.
- **Intellectual Property:** Any future patents or commercialization will be disclosed transparently in the `intellectual_property.md` file.

## 2. Funding Statement
- **Current Funding:** This research is currently unfunded and is being developed independently as an open-source project.
- **Future Funding:** If the project receives grants (e.g., from the Khwarizmi Award or university grants), the funding sources will be explicitly acknowledged in all publications and project documentation.

## 3. Research Integrity & Data Authenticity
- **No Fabrication:** All data presented in our simulations and future clinical trials will be real. Synthetic data will be clearly labeled as such.
- **No Plagiarism:** All external code, algorithms, and literature are properly cited in `references.md`. The AI tools used for coding assistance are acknowledged transparently.
- **Reproducibility:** The entire pipeline is open-source on GitHub, allowing independent researchers to reproduce our results.

## 4. Ethical Approval
- Any research involving human subjects (e.g., the pilot clinical trial) will be conducted only after receiving formal approval from the relevant Institutional Review Board (IRB) and will adhere strictly to the Declaration of Helsinki.
