# Next-Generation Biosensors for Future Expansion

This document outlines how the BioSignal platform can evolve by integrating next-generation biosensing technologies.

## 1. From Electrical Signals to Biomarkers
Currently, BioSignal focuses on electrical signals (ECG). To expand into early disease detection (like cancer or diabetes), we will need to integrate **electrochemical biosensors**.
- **Concept:** Using functionalized nanoparticles or antibodies to detect specific proteins (biomarkers) in sweat or interstitial fluid.
- **Application:** Detecting early signs of breast cancer (e.g., CA 15-3 biomarker) or continuous glucose monitoring for diabetic patients.

## 2. Flexible and Stretchable Electronics
- **Concept:** Moving from rigid PCBs to flexible substrates (like polyimide or PDMS).
- **Benefit:** This allows the patch to conform perfectly to the patient's skin, reducing motion artifacts and improving comfort.

## 3. Self-Powered Sensors (Energy Harvesting)
- **Concept:** Instead of relying solely on a battery, the device can harvest energy from the human body.
- **Technologies:** 
  - **Thermoelectric Generators:** Converting body heat into electricity.
  - **Piezoelectric Materials:** Generating power from the mechanical movement of the chest during breathing.

## 4. AI-Driven Multi-Modal Monitoring
- By combining electrical signals (ECG) with optical signals (PPG) and biochemical markers (sweat), the TinyML model can predict complex conditions like sepsis or heart failure before clinical symptoms appear.

## Conclusion
The future of BioSignal is not just a heart monitor; it is a comprehensive, self-powered, multi-modal health platform.
