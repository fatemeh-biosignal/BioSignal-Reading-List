# Social and Economic Impact

This document outlines the broader impact of the BioSignal project on society and the healthcare system.

## 1. Healthcare Impact
- **Early Detection Saves Lives:** By continuously monitoring high-risk patients, BioSignal can detect life-threatening arrhythmias hours or days before a critical event (like a stroke or heart attack).
- **Reducing Hospital Burden:** Continuous home monitoring reduces the need for frequent hospital visits, freeing up hospital beds and reducing the workload on medical staff.

## 2. Economic Impact
- **Cost Reduction:** The low-cost design (~$33) makes it affordable for the public healthcare system. Preventing a stroke is far cheaper than treating one.
- **Job Creation:** If commercialized, the project can create jobs in the fields of hardware manufacturing, software development, and clinical support.

## 3. Social Impact
- **Peace of Mind for Families:** The alert system notifies both the patient and their family members, providing reassurance that help is always available.
- **Accessibility for Rural Areas:** Since the device is portable and can run without a constant internet connection (due to on-device TinyML), it is perfect for patients in remote or underserved areas.

## 4. Alignment with Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
This project aligns with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goal #3: **Good Health and Well-being**.
