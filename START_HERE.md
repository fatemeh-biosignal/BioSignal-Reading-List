# 👋 Welcome to My Research Journey

If you are visiting this repository for the first time, here is a quick guide on how to navigate it.

## 🎯 What is this project about?
I am designing a **low-power wearable ECG system** that uses **Compressive Sensing (CS)** and **TinyML** to detect heart arrhythmias (specifically Atrial Fibrillation) in real-time. This is a bridge between my background in Electrical Engineering and my future in Medicine.

## 🗺️ How to navigate this repository

1. **Start with the Idea:** Read the [`project-idea.md`](project-idea.md) to understand the problem and the proposed solution.
2. **Learn the Theory:** Check [`concepts.md`](concepts.md) for the technical and medical foundations (CS, TinyML, AFib).
3. **See the Code:** Look at the `.py` files (`ecg_simulation.py`, `compressed_sensing_demo.py`, `reconstruction_demo.py`) to see how the algorithms are simulated.
4. **Read My Progress:** The [`notes.md`](notes.md) file shows my daily progress and what I am learning.
5. **Explore the Research:** The [`reading-log.md`](reading-log.md) contains summaries of scientific papers I have studied.
