# Wearable Technology Comparison

This document compares the BioSignal ECG patch with other emerging wearable technologies, such as smart rings and smartwatches.

## 1. The BioSignal Approach (ECG Patch)
- **Sensing Modality:** Electrical (Biopotential).
- **Primary Metric:** Heart Rate & Arrhythmia Detection (AFib).
- **Form Factor:** Adhesive chest patch.
- **Advantages:** High clinical accuracy (Gold standard for heart rhythm).
- **Challenges:** Requires skin contact/adhesive, motion artifacts.

## 2. Emerging Tech: Smart Rings (e.g., USC Research)
- **Sensing Modality:** Optical (PPG, Near-Infrared for sweat analysis).
- **Primary Metric:** Heart Rate, Stress Levels (via sweat/optodermal activity), Temperature.
- **Form Factor:** Ring on the finger.
- **Advantages:** No electrodes/gels needed, very comfortable for continuous wear, excellent for psychological stress detection.
- **Challenges:** PPG is highly susceptible to motion artifacts and poor blood perfusion in cold fingers. Cannot accurately detect arrhythmias like AFib.

## 3. Smartwatches (e.g., Apple Watch, Samsung)
- **Sensing Modality:** Optical (PPG) + Electrical (Single-lead ECG).
- **Primary Metric:** General wellness, step count, basic HR alerts.
- **Advantages:** High user adoption, multi-functional.
- **Challenges:** Battery life is short (1-2 days), single-lead ECG is less accurate than a continuous patch.

## 4. Key Takeaways for BioSignal
To stay competitive, BioSignal can integrate **multi-modal sensing** in the future:
- Adding a **PPG sensor** to the patch to monitor SpO2 and stress levels.
- Utilizing **flexible PCB stacks** (as seen in the smart ring) to make the patch even thinner and more comfortable.
