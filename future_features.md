# Future Features & Enhancements

This document outlines potential features for future versions of the BioSignal device (Version 2.0 and beyond).

## 1. Advanced AI Features
- **Multi-Class Arrhythmia Detection:** Moving beyond binary classification (Normal vs. Arrhythmia) to detect specific types of arrhythmias (e.g., AFib, PVC, PAC).
- **Patient-Specific Baseline:** The AI learns the patient's normal heart rhythm over time and alerts them only if there is a significant deviation.

## 2. Hardware Enhancements
- **Multi-Sensor Integration:** Adding a PPG (Photoplethysmography) sensor to monitor blood oxygen levels (SpO2) and blood pressure trends.
- **Solar Charging:** Integrating a flexible solar cell into the patch to extend battery life indefinitely.

## 3. Mobile App Features
- **Telemedicine Integration:** A built-in video call feature in the app to connect the patient directly with a cardiologist.
- **Family Sharing:** Allowing family members to receive alerts if the patient is in danger.

## 4. Research & Development
- **Federated Learning:** Updating the AI model across all devices without sharing raw patient data, preserving privacy while improving accuracy.
- **Expansion to Other Diseases:** Using the same hardware platform to detect early signs of sleep apnea or stress levels.
