# User Personas

This document defines the target users of the BioSignal device to ensure the design meets their real-world needs.

## Persona 1: The High-Risk Elderly Patient
- **Name:** Mr. Reza (72 years old)
- **Background:** Has a history of atrial fibrillation and lives alone.
- **Needs:** Continuous monitoring without having to learn complex technology. He needs a device that is comfortable to wear 24/7 and sends alerts to his family if something goes wrong.
- **Pain Points:** Finds hospital visits exhausting. Forgets to take medication.
- **BioSignal Solution:** The ultra-thin patch is comfortable. The automated BLE alerts notify his son's phone immediately if an arrhythmia occurs.

## Persona 2: The Busy Cardiologist
- **Name:** Dr. Sara (45 years old)
- **Background:** Works at a busy hospital in Zanjan, seeing 30+ patients a day.
- **Needs:** A reliable system to monitor high-risk patients remotely. She needs the AI to filter out normal heartbeats so she only receives alerts for critical events.
- **Pain Points:** Suffers from "alarm fatigue" due to too many false positives from existing monitors.
- **BioSignal Solution:** The TinyML model pre-screens the data on the device. Dr. Sara only receives an alert if the AI detects a genuine anomaly, saving her time.

## Persona 3: The Tech-Savvy Family Member
- **Name:** Ali (35 years old)
- **Background:** Lives in Tehran, while his parents live in Zanjan.
- **Needs:** Peace of mind regarding his father's heart condition.
- **BioSignal Solution:** The mobile app allows Ali to view his father's heart rate trends and receive emergency alerts, bridging the geographical distance.
