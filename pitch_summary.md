# 🚀 BioSignal: The Future of Wearable ECG

## 🎯 The Problem
Cardiovascular diseases are the #1 cause of death globally. Continuous monitoring is limited by battery life and data privacy.

## 💡 Our Solution
A low-power wearable ECG patch that uses **Compressive Sensing** to reduce data size by 70%, and **TinyML** for on-device Arrhythmia detection.

## 🏆 Key Features
- **Ultra-Low Power:** Extended battery life (up to 1 week).
- **Privacy First:** AI runs on the device; no raw data is sent to the cloud.
- **Real-Time Alerts:** Detects Bradycardia and Tachycardia instantly.

## 📈 Market Potential
- **Target:** Hospitals, elderly care, and home healthcare.
- **Cost:** Affordable for developing countries (using low-cost STM32/AD8232 components).

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- Python (Simulation), C (Embedded), Mermaid (Documentation), Scikit-Learn (AI).

## 📅 Roadmap
- **Now:** Algorithm simulation and GUI dashboard.
- **Next:** Hardware prototyping (PCB design).
- **Future:** Clinical trials and commercialization.
