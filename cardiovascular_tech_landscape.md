# Cardiovascular Technology Landscape

This document provides an overview of the current landscape of cardiovascular medical technologies, positioning BioSignal within the broader ecosystem.

## 1. Tier 1: Prevention & Monitoring (BioSignal's Domain)
- **Technologies:** Wearable ECG patches, Holter monitors, Smartwatches (Apple, Samsung).
- **Goal:** Early detection of arrhythmias (e.g., AFib) and continuous monitoring.
- **BioSignal's Position:** By combining Compressive Sensing and TinyML, BioSignal offers a low-cost, privacy-preserving, and long-lasting monitoring solution.

## 2. Tier 2: Mechanical Circulatory Support (VADs)
- **Technologies:** Left Ventricular Assist Devices (LVADs).
- **Goal:** Helping a failing heart pump blood without replacing it entirely.
- **Example:** HeartMate 3.

## 3. Tier 3: Total Artificial Hearts (TAH)
- **Technologies:** Devices that completely replace the patient's native heart.
- **Goal:** Bridge-to-transplant or destination therapy for end-stage heart failure.
- **Example:** CARMAT Aeson® (France). It mimics natural pulsatile blood flow and automatically adjusts to the body's needs.

## 4. The Future: Artificial Organs & Bionics
As medical engineering evolves, the line between machine and biology blurs. In the future, BioSignal's AI algorithms could potentially be integrated into closed-loop systems, where the monitoring device communicates directly with a mechanical assist device (like a VAD or TAH) to adjust the heart's output automatically.
