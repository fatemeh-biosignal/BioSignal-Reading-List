# Future MedTech Trends (Targeted Drug Delivery & Robotics)

Inspired by recent breakthroughs at ETH Zürich (magnetic microrobots for stroke treatment), this document outlines the future trends in Medical Technology and how BioSignal can integrate with them.

## 1. Targeted Drug Delivery (رساندن هدفمند دارو)
- **Current Paradigm:** Systemic drug delivery (e.g., taking a pill) affects the whole body and can cause severe side effects (like bleeding when taking clot-dissolving drugs).
- **Future Trend:** Using magnetic microrobots (< 2 mm) or nanoparticles guided by external magnetic fields to deliver medication *exactly* to the diseased tissue (e.g., a blood clot in the brain).
- **Benefit:** Maximum efficacy with minimal systemic toxicity.

## 2. Closed-Loop Healthcare (سلامت حلقه‌بسته)
The future is not just about monitoring or treating; it is about combining them.
- **Step 1 (Monitor):** BioSignal (or future wearable sensors) detects an early sign of a stroke (e.g., AFib or abnormal heart rhythm).
- **Step 2 (Alert):** The AI sends an immediate alert to the hospital.
- **Step 3 (Intervene):** The medical team uses targeted drug delivery (like the ETH microrobots) to dissolve the clot before permanent damage occurs.

## 3. BioSignal's Role in this Future
While BioSignal currently focuses on early detection via ECG, its AI and data processing architecture can be adapted to:
- Predict the risk of stroke based on long-term cardiac data.
- Communicate with closed-loop drug delivery systems in the hospital.
- Monitor the patient's recovery post-intervention.
