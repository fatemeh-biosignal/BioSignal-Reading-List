# Digital Pathology & Future Diagnostics Roadmap

This document outlines the long-term vision of the founder (Fatemeh Karimi) to bridge the gap between bio-signal processing (BioSignal) and digital pathology, ultimately creating a multi-modal diagnostic platform.

## 1. The Founder's Vision: From Signals to Cells
While BioSignal focuses on early screening of cardiovascular diseases via ECG, the ultimate goal is to expand into **Digital Pathology**. After completing medical school and specializing in pathology, the founder aims to apply the same AI and engineering principles to analyze tissue samples (Whole Slide Images - WSI).

## 2. How BioSignal's Tech Translates to Pathology
The engineering skills developed in BioSignal are highly transferable:
- **Signal Processing (ECG) -> Image Processing (WSI):** The Butterworth filter and wavelet transforms used for ECG can be adapted for image denoising and enhancement in pathology slides.
- **Compressive Sensing -> Image Compression:** CS can be used to compress massive pathology slide images for faster transmission between hospitals.
- **TinyML -> Edge AI in Pathology:** While pathology requires high-end GPUs, lightweight models (similar to our TinyML) can be deployed on portable microscopes for remote diagnosis in underserved areas.

## 3. The Future: A Multi-Modal Health Platform
The future of diagnostics is **Multi-Modal**. Instead of isolated tests, a patient's health will be assessed by combining:
1. **BioSignal (ECG):** Continuous cardiac monitoring.
2. **Wearable Biosensors (PPG/EDA):** Stress and sleep analysis.
3. **Digital Pathology (AI-assisted):** Automated detection of cancer cells in biopsy images.
4. **Genomic Data:** Personalized medicine based on DNA analysis.

## 4. Conclusion
BioSignal is just the beginning. It represents the founder's commitment to using technology to solve complex medical problems, from the heart to the cellular level.
