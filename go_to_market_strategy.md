# Go-to-Market (GTM) Strategy

This document outlines the strategy for launching and commercializing the BioSignal device.

## 1. Phase 1: Pilot Testing (B2B Focus)
- **Target:** 2-3 major hospitals in Tehran and Zanjan (e.g., specialized heart hospitals).
- **Strategy:** Provide free prototypes to cardiology departments for a 3-month pilot study.
- **Goal:** Collect real-world clinical data and obtain testimonial letters from doctors.

## 2. Phase 2: Early Adoption (B2C & B2B)
- **Target:** High-risk patients and private cardiology clinics.
- **Channel:** Direct sales through the BioSignal website and partnerships with medical equipment distributors.
- **Pricing:** A subsidized hardware cost (e.g., $20) with a monthly subscription fee (e.g., $5) for the AI cloud analytics and doctor dashboard.

## 3. Phase 3: Mass Market & Government Partnerships
- **Target:** Ministry of Health and public health programs.
- **Strategy:** Bid for government tenders to provide remote monitoring for elderly patients in rural areas.
- **Goal:** Become the standard of care for remote cardiac monitoring in Iran.

## 4. Marketing Channels
- **Medical Conferences:** Present the device at the Iranian Conference on Electrical Engineering (ICEE) and medical expos.
- **Digital Marketing:** Targeted social media campaigns (Instagram/LinkedIn) focused on heart health awareness.
- **KOLs (Key Opinion Leaders):** Partner with well-known cardiologists to endorse the product.
