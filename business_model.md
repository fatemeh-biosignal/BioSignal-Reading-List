# Business Model Canvas

This document outlines the business model for the wearable ECG device.

## 1. Value Proposition (ارزش پیشنهادی)
- A low-cost, low-power wearable ECG patch.
- Continuous monitoring without the need for frequent battery changes.
- Privacy-preserving AI (no data sent to the cloud).

## 2. Customer Segments (مشتریان)
- **B2B:** Hospitals, clinics, and elderly care homes.
- **B2C:** Patients with a history of heart disease, and health-conscious individuals.

## 3. Revenue Streams (منابع درآمد)
- **Hardware Sales:** Selling the wearable patch and the base station.
- **Subscription Model:** A monthly fee for the mobile app's advanced analytics and doctor consultations.
- **Data Licensing:** (Anonymized data) for medical research (with user consent).

## 4. Cost Structure (ساختار هزینه)
- R&D (Research and Development).
- Manufacturing and component costs.
- Marketing and distribution.
