# Regulatory Pathway

This document outlines the regulatory requirements to commercialize the BioSignal wearable ECG device in Iran and internationally.

## 1. Iran (Ministry of Health - IRC)
- **Authority:** Food and Drug Administration of Iran (IFDA).
- **Classification:** Class II Medical Device (Active diagnostic device).
- **Requirements:**
  - ISO 13485 (Quality Management System).
  - Clinical evaluation report (CER).
  - Electrical safety and EMC testing (IEC 60601-1).

## 2. Europe (CE Marking)
- **Regulation:** Medical Device Regulation (MDR 2017/745).
- **Classification:** Class IIa.
- **Requirements:**
  - Conformity assessment by a Notified Body.
  - Post-market surveillance (PMS) plan.

## 3. United States (FDA)
- **Authority:** Food and Drug Administration.
- **Pathway:** 510(k) Premarket Notification.
- **Requirements:**
  - Demonstration of substantial equivalence to a legally marketed predicate device (e.g., existing Holter monitors).

## 4. Next Steps
- Implement a Quality Management System (QMS) based on ISO 13485.
- Partner with a certified testing lab for IEC 60601-1 compliance.
