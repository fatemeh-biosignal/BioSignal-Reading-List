# Product Packaging & Unboxing Experience

This document outlines the design of the physical packaging and the user unboxing experience.

## 1. Box Design
- **Material:** Eco-friendly, recycled cardboard.
- **Size:** Compact (similar to a smartphone box) to reduce shipping costs.
- **Branding:** Minimalist design with the BioSignal logo and a subtle ECG heartbeat line.

## 2. Box Contents
When the patient opens the box, they will find:
1. **The BioSignal Core Device:** The reusable electronic module.
2. **Disposable Patch Pack:** A box containing 4 patches (enough for one month).
3. **Charging Dock:** A sleek, USB-C magnetic charging dock.
4. **Quick Start Guide:** A colorful, step-by-step visual guide (with QR code to the mobile app).
5. **Warranty Card:** Information about the 1-year warranty and customer support.

## 3. Unboxing Journey (UX)
1. **First Impression:** The user sees the sleek device and the elegant packaging.
2. **Setup:** They follow the visual guide to download the app and pair the device via BLE.
3. **First Use:** They apply the first patch and see their real-time heart rate on the app within 2 minutes.
4. **Emotional Connection:** The app welcomes them with a reassuring message: "We are here to keep your heart safe."

## 4. Regulatory Labels
- The box will include the CE mark, FDA clearance (future), and the IRC mark (Iran).
- A warning label for patients with pacemakers.
