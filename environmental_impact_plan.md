# Environmental Impact & Sustainability Plan

This document outlines the strategy for minimizing the environmental footprint of the BioSignal device, adhering to the principles of Green MedTech.

## 1. The Challenge of E-Waste in Wearables
Disposable ECG patches contain plastics, adhesives, and electronic components. If not disposed of properly, they contribute to the growing global e-waste problem.

## 2. Sustainable Design Principles
- **Biodegradable Adhesives:** We will source medical-grade adhesives that are biocompatible but break down faster in landfills compared to traditional acrylics.
- **Recyclable PCB:** The rigid part of the patch is designed to be easily detached from the flexible electrode. The PCB (containing the STM32 and BLE) can be sent to specialized e-waste recycling facilities.
- **Minimal Packaging:** Using 100% recycled cardboard and soy-based inks for the product box.

## 3. Battery Disposal & Recycling
- **Li-Po Batteries:** The battery must never be thrown in regular trash. The BioSignal app will guide users to the nearest battery recycling drop-off point.
- **Future Tech:** Researching the integration of solid-state batteries or energy-harvesting (piezoelectric) materials to eliminate chemical batteries entirely.

## 4. Life Cycle Assessment (LCA)
- A full LCA will be conducted after the commercial launch to measure the carbon footprint of the device, from raw material extraction to final disposal.
- The results will be published transparently to encourage other MedTech startups to adopt sustainable practices.
