# Clinical Trial Protocol (Pilot Study)

This document outlines the protocol for the initial pilot study to validate the BioSignal wearable ECG device against standard clinical equipment.

## 1. Study Objectives
- **Primary Objective:** To evaluate the accuracy (sensitivity and specificity) of the BioSignal device in detecting arrhythmias compared to a standard 12-lead ECG or Holter monitor.
- **Secondary Objective:** To assess patient comfort and device usability over a 7-day period.

## 2. Study Design
- **Type:** Prospective, single-center, observational study.
- **Location:** A collaborating hospital in Zanjan, Iran (e.g., Ayatollah Mousavi Hospital).
- **Duration:** 3 months (recruitment and data collection).
- **Sample Size:** 20 patients with known or suspected cardiac arrhythmias.

## 3. Inclusion & Exclusion Criteria
- **Inclusion:** Adults (18-75 years) with a history of palpitations or diagnosed arrhythmias.
- **Exclusion:** Patients with severe skin allergies (to adhesives) or those with implanted pacemakers (unless cleared by a cardiologist).

## 4. Outcome Measures
- **Sensitivity:** The ability of BioSignal to correctly identify true arrhythmias.
- **Specificity:** The ability of BioSignal to correctly identify normal heartbeats.
- **Adverse Events:** Monitoring for skin irritation or device malfunctions.

## 5. Ethical Considerations
- **Informed Consent:** All patients must sign a detailed consent form explaining the study and data privacy.
- **Data Privacy:** Patient data will be anonymized and encrypted (AES-128) in compliance with national health regulations.
- **Ethics Committee:** The protocol will be submitted to the Institutional Review Board (IRB) of Zanjan University of Medical Sciences for approval.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities
- **Principal Investigator (PI):** Fatemeh Karimi (Project Lead).
- **Medical Supervisor:** A licensed cardiologist at the collaborating hospital.
- **Data Manager:** Responsible for extracting and analyzing the anonymized ECG data.
