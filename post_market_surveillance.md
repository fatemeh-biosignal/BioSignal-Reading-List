# Post-Market Surveillance (PMS) & Vigilance Plan

This document outlines the strategy for monitoring the safety and performance of the BioSignal device after it has been released to the market (ISO 13485 requirement).

## 1. Objectives of PMS
- To ensure the continued safety and effectiveness of the device.
- To detect any unforeseen side effects or device malfunctions (e.g., skin irritation, battery swelling, false alarms).
- To update the AI model based on real-world data.

## 2. Data Collection Methods
- **User Feedback:** In-app reporting system for patients and doctors to report issues.
- **Remote Telemetry:** Anonymous, aggregated data on device crashes and battery performance (with user consent).
- **Literature Review:** Monitoring new research on ECG patch adhesives and AI safety.

## 3. Incident Reporting & Vigilance
If a serious incident occurs (e.g., the device fails to alert a patient during a severe arrhythmia):
1. The patient/doctor reports the incident via the app or customer support.
2. The Quality Assurance (QA) team investigates the root cause within 48 hours.
3. If it is a systemic issue, a **Field Safety Corrective Action (FSCA)** is initiated (e.g., a firmware update or product recall).
4. The incident is reported to the regulatory body (e.g., IFDA in Iran) within the legally required timeframe.

## 4. Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA)
- Every reported issue is logged in our CAPA system.
- **Corrective Action:** Fixing the immediate problem (e.g., replacing a faulty battery).
- **Preventive Action:** Modifying the design or manufacturing process to ensure the issue never happens again (e.g., changing the battery supplier).

## 5. Periodic Safety Update Reports (PSUR)
- An annual report will be generated, summarizing the PMS data, incident reports, and CAPA effectiveness.
- This report will be submitted to the regulatory authorities to maintain the device's market approval.
