# User Manual (دفترچه راهنمای کاربر)

Welcome to BioSignal! This guide will help you set up and use your wearable ECG monitoring device.

## 1. Intended Use (موارد استفاده)
This device is intended for continuous monitoring of heart rate and early detection of arrhythmias (like Atrial Fibrillation) in home or hospital settings.

## 2. Device Components (اجزای دستگاه)
- **ECG Patch:** The wearable sensor that sticks to your chest.
- **Charging Dock:** For wireless charging of the patch.
- **Mobile App:** For viewing real-time heart rate and receiving alerts.

## 3. How to Wear (نحوه استفاده)
1. Clean and dry your chest area.
2. Remove the protective film from the ECG patch.
3. Apply the patch firmly to your left chest (near the heart).
4. Open the BioSignal mobile app and connect via Bluetooth.

## 4. Understanding the Alerts (درک هشدارها)
- 🟢 **Normal:** Your heart rate is between 60 and 100 BPM.
- ⚠️ **Bradycardia Alert:** Your heart rate is below 60 BPM. Please sit down and rest.
- 🚨 **Tachycardia/Arrhythmia Alert:** Your heart rate is above 100 BPM or an irregular rhythm is detected. Please contact your doctor immediately.

## 5. Charging & Maintenance (شارژ و نگهداری)
- Battery life is approximately 3 days.
- When the battery is low, the app will notify you. Place the patch on the charging dock for 2 hours.
- Do not submerge the charging dock in water.

## 6. Troubleshooting (عیب‌یابی)
- **Bluetooth not connecting:** Ensure Bluetooth is enabled on your phone and the device is within 10 meters.
- **Skin irritation:** Remove the patch and consult a doctor. Do not reapply to the same spot immediately.
