# Team Structure & Roles

This document outlines the required roles and team structure to execute the BioSignal project successfully.

## 1. Current Team (Phase 1)
- **Fatemeh Karimi (Founder & Lead Researcher):** Responsible for the overall vision, algorithm design, software simulation, and project management.

## 2. Required Roles for Future Phases
To move from simulation to a commercial product, the following roles will be recruited:
- **Hardware Engineer:** Responsible for PCB design (Altium), component selection, and hardware testing.
- **AI/ML Engineer:** Responsible for optimizing the TinyML model and deploying it to the STM32 microcontroller.
- **Medical Advisor (Cardiologist):** A licensed cardiologist to oversee clinical trials and ensure medical validity.
- **Regulatory Consultant:** An expert to guide the IRC and CE/FDA approval process.
- **Business Developer:** To handle fundraising, marketing, and hospital partnerships.

## 3. Organizational Structure
- **CEO:** Fatemeh Karimi
- **CTO (Chief Technology Officer):** Hardware Engineer + AI Engineer
- **CMO (Chief Medical Officer):** Medical Advisor
- **COO (Chief Operating Officer):** Business Developer

## 4. Collaboration Tools
- **GitHub:** For code and documentation.
- **Notion/Trello:** For task management and sprint planning.
- **Slack/Telegram:** For daily communication.
