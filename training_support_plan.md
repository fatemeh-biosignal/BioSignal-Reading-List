# Healthcare Professional Training & Support Plan

This document outlines the strategy for training medical staff and providing customer support for the BioSignal system.

## 1. Target Audience
- **Cardiologists:** Need to learn how to interpret the AI alerts and review the reconstructed ECG signals.
- **Nurses:** Need to learn how to apply the patch and check for skin irritation.
- **Patients (End-users):** Need to learn how to pair the device with their phone and charge it.

## 2. Training Materials
- **Video Tutorials:** Short 3-minute videos in Persian and English demonstrating how to use the app and apply the patch.
- **Quick Start Guide:** A one-page printed guide included in the product box.
- **Webinars:** Monthly online training sessions for hospital staff to answer their questions.

## 3. Customer Support Tiers
### Tier 1: Patient Support (24/7)
- **Channel:** In-app chat and toll-free phone number.
- **Scope:** Troubleshooting Bluetooth connection, battery charging, and skin irritation issues.

### Tier 2: Clinical Support (9 AM - 5 PM)
- **Channel:** Dedicated email and phone line for hospitals.
- **Scope:** Technical issues with the doctor's dashboard, data synchronization, and firmware updates.

### Tier 3: Engineering Support (Escalation)
- **Channel:** Direct contact with the BioSignal engineering team.
- **Scope:** Hardware failures, AI model anomalies, and cybersecurity incidents.

## 4. Feedback Loop
- All support tickets will be categorized and analyzed monthly.
- Frequent issues will be addressed in the next firmware update or documented in the FAQ section of the app.
