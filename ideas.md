# Project Brainstorming (ایده‌های جدید)

Here are some simple ideas to explore for my wearable ECG project in the future:
- Idea 1: Adding a warning alarm that vibrates when the heart rate is too high.
- Idea 2: Making the device patch-like (like a band-aid) so the patient can wear it comfortably under clothes.
- Idea 3: Implementing a feature that shares the ECG data with the patient's family in an emergency.
- Idea 4: Using solar energy or body heat to charge the battery!
