# Authorship & Contribution Guidelines

This document outlines the principles for attributing authorship and recognizing contributions to the BioSignal project.

## 1. Authorship Criteria
To be listed as an author on any paper (e.g., ICEE 2027) or patent derived from this project, an individual must meet all the following criteria (based on ICMJE guidelines):
- **Substantial contributions:** Making a significant intellectual contribution to the conception, design, or execution of the project.
- **Drafting or revising:** Participating in drafting the manuscript or revising it critically for important intellectual content.
- **Final approval:** Providing final approval of the version to be published.
- **Accountability:** Agreeing to be accountable for all aspects of the work.

## 3. Acknowledgments
Individuals who contribute to the project but do not meet the authorship criteria (e.g., providing technical support, data collection, or general supervision) will be acknowledged in the "Acknowledgments" section of the paper.

## 4. Future Collaborators
If a cardiologist or hardware engineer joins the project in the future, their specific contributions will be documented in this file, and their authorship will be negotiated based on the ICMJE criteria.
