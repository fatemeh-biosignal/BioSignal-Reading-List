# Fundraising Strategy

This document outlines how the BioSignal project will secure funding to move from a prototype to a commercial product.

## 1. Pre-Seed Stage (Current Phase)
- **Goal:** Build the first physical prototype (PCB + Enclosure).
- **Amount Needed:** ~$5,000 (Approx. 300 million IRR).
- **Source of Funds:** 
  - Personal savings.
  - University grants (e.g., University of Zanjan research grants).
  - National competitions (e.g., Khwarizmi Award prize money).

## 2. Seed Stage (Next Phase)
- **Goal:** Conduct clinical trials and obtain initial regulatory approvals (IRC).
- **Amount Needed:** ~$50,000.
- **Source of Funds:**
  - **Venture Capital (VC):** Iranian VC firms specializing in MedTech (e.g., Sarava, Shasta).
  - **Government Grants:** Innovation and Prosperity Fund (صندوق نوآوری و شکوفایی).

## 3. Series A (Commercialization)
- **Goal:** Mass production and market entry.
- **Amount Needed:** ~$500,000.
- **Source of Funds:**
  - Series A funding from larger investors.
  - Strategic partnership with established medical device manufacturers (e.g., Pooyandegan Rah Saadat).

## 4. Key Success Factors for Investors
Investors will look for:
- **Clinical Validation:** Real-world data proving the device saves lives.
- **Regulatory Approval:** The IRC certificate.
- **Scalable Business Model:** The subscription and hardware revenue streams.
