# Informed Consent Form (ICF) Template

**Study Title:** Pilot Study for the Validation of the BioSignal Wearable ECG Device
**Principal Investigator:** Fatemeh Karimi
**Institution:** Independent Research (in collaboration with [Hospital Name])

## 1. Introduction
You are being invited to take part in a research study. Before you decide whether to participate, it is important for you to understand why the research is being done and what it will involve. Please take time to read the following information carefully.

## 2. Purpose of the Study
The purpose of this study is to test the accuracy and comfort of a new wearable ECG patch (BioSignal) designed to monitor heart rhythms and detect arrhythmias (irregular heartbeats).

## 3. What will happen to you?
If you agree to take part, a small, adhesive patch will be placed on your chest. You will wear this patch for 7 days. During this time, the device will continuously monitor your heart's electrical activity. You will need to keep a diary of your daily activities and any symptoms (e.g., palpitations or dizziness) using our mobile app.

## 4. Risks and Discomforts
- **Skin Irritation:** You may experience mild redness or itching where the patch is applied. If this occurs, the patch will be removed.
- **Privacy:** Your data is encrypted and anonymized. No personal identifiers will be shared in any publication.

## 5. Benefits
There may be no direct benefit to you from this study. However, the information we get from this study may help us develop a better, more affordable heart monitoring device for patients in the future.

## 6. Confidentiality
All data collected during this study will be kept strictly confidential. Data will be stored on secure, encrypted servers in compliance with national health regulations.

## 7. Voluntary Participation
Your participation is entirely voluntary. You are free to withdraw from the study at any time without giving a reason and without your medical care being affected.

## 8. Consent Statement
