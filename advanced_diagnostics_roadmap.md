# Advanced Diagnostics Roadmap

This document outlines the long-term vision for expanding the BioSignal platform into advanced, multi-modal medical diagnostics.

## 1. The Evolution of BioSignal
While the current focus of BioSignal is on early screening of cardiovascular diseases via ECG, the underlying technology (AI, signal processing, and low-power hardware) serves as a foundation for broader medical applications.

## 2. Expanding to Image Analysis
The mathematical principles used in BioSignal (such as wavelet transforms and Compressive Sensing) are highly transferable to **Medical Image Analysis**:
- **Image Denoising:** Algorithms similar to our Butterworth filter can be adapted for denoising MRI or CT scans.
- **Data Compression:** CS can be used to compress massive medical images for faster transmission between hospitals.
- **Edge AI:** Lightweight models (similar to TinyML) can be deployed on portable microscopes for remote diagnosis in underserved areas.

## 3. The Future: A Multi-Modal Health Platform
The future of diagnostics lies in combining different data sources to get a holistic view of a patient's health:
1. **BioSignal (ECG):** Continuous cardiac monitoring.
2. **Wearable Biosensors (PPG/EDA):** Stress and sleep analysis.
3. **AI-Assisted Imaging:** Automated detection of anomalies in medical scans.
4. **Genomic Data:** Personalized medicine based on DNA analysis.

## 4. Conclusion
BioSignal is just the beginning. It represents a commitment to using engineering and AI to solve complex medical problems across different specialties, always with a focus on patient privacy and accessibility.
