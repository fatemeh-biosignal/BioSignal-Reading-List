# Technology Readiness Level (TRL)

This document defines the current maturity of the BioSignal project using the NASA/European Commission TRL scale.

## Current TRL Status: Level 4 (Lab Validation)
- **TRL 1-3 (Basic Research & Proof of Concept):** The mathematical foundations of Compressive Sensing and the TinyML algorithms have been established. (Completed)
- **TRL 4 (Component Validation in Lab):** The software pipeline (Python) has been fully simulated, and the hardware architecture (Schematic, Pinout) has been designed. (Current Status)

## Roadmap to TRL 9 (Commercial Product)
- **TRL 5 (Validation in Relevant Environment):** Build the physical PCB prototype and test the electrical safety and filtering. (Next Phase)
- **TRL 6 (Prototype Demonstration):** Test the device on 10 healthy volunteers and compare with hospital ECG.
- **TRL 7 (System Prototype in Operational Environment):** Conduct a pilot study on 20 patients in a hospital (Zanjan).
- **TRL 8 (System Complete and Qualified):** Obtain IRC (FDA of Iran) regulatory approval.
- **TRL 9 (Actual System Proven in Operational Environment):** Mass production and commercial launch.

## Conclusion
The BioSignal project has successfully passed the basic research phase (TRL 1-3) and is currently at **TRL 4**. The next critical step is hardware prototyping and clinical validation.
