# Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) & Neuroethics Roadmap

Inspired by recent breakthroughs in dream decoding (e.g., ATR Computational Neuroscience Labs, Japan), this document outlines the future roadmap for integrating brain-computer interfaces into the BioSignal platform and addressing the ethical challenges.

## 1. The Next Frontier: From ECG to EEG
While BioSignal currently focuses on cardiovascular health (ECG), the ultimate wearable health platform will include **Electroencephalography (EEG)** to monitor brain activity.
- **Applications:** Sleep staging, seizure detection (epilepsy), and early detection of neurodegenerative diseases (e.g., Alzheimer's).
- **Hardware Evolution:** Moving from wet electrodes (used in clinics) to dry, flexible EEG sensors integrated into a headband or behind-the-ear device.

## 2. AI & Dream Decoding
Research shows that AI can decode visual imagery and dreams using fMRI data. For a wearable device, this translates to:
- **Decoding mental states:** Detecting high cognitive load, emotional stress, or fatigue.
- **Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCI):** Allowing paralyzed patients to control prosthetics or communicate via thought.

## 3. The Neuroethics Challenge (اخلاق عصب‌شناسی)
As we move towards AI that can "read" the brain, we must establish strict ethical boundaries:
- **Mental Privacy (حریم خصوصی ذهنی):** A person's thoughts and dreams must be protected. The device must never record or transmit raw brain data without explicit, continuous consent.
- **Cognitive Liberty:** Individuals have the right to control their own mental states. The device must not be used to manipulate emotions or behavior.
- **Data Security:** Brain data is the most sensitive data possible. It must be heavily encrypted (AES-256) and processed entirely on-device (Edge AI).

## 4. BioSignal's Pledge
If BioSignal ever expands into brain monitoring, our core principle will be: **"Monitor to Heal, Never to Control."** All algorithms will be open-source, and any data collection will be strictly opt-in and anonymized.
