# Publication Guide (ICEE 2027)

This document outlines the guidelines and standards for preparing the manuscript for the ICEE 2027 conference.

## 1. Paper Structure
The paper will follow the standard IEEE conference template:
- **Title & Authors:** Fatemeh Karimi (Independent Researcher).
- **Abstract:** Max 250 words summarizing the problem, method, and results.
- **Introduction:** Background, problem statement, and contributions.
- **Methodology:** Hardware (AD8232, STM32), CS, and TinyML.
- **Results:** MSE comparison, classification accuracy, and cost analysis.
- **Conclusion & Future Work:** Summary and next steps.
- **References:** IEEE format (as documented in `references.md`).

## 2. Formatting Rules
- **Template:** IEEE Conference Template (A4 paper size).
- **Font:** Times New Roman, 10 pt (for body text).
- **Page Limit:** Maximum of 6 pages (including references).
- **Figures:** High-resolution (300 dpi), placed within the text, with captions below.

## 3. Submission Checklist
- [ ] Paper is written in clear, academic English.
- [ ] All figures and tables are referenced in the text.
- [ ] Plagiarism check completed (similarity < 10%).
- [ ] PDF is generated using the IEEE PDF eXpress tool.
- [ ] Submission completed via the ICEE online portal before the deadline.

## 4. Post-Submission
- Prepare a 10-minute presentation (using `presentation_outline.md`).
- Prepare the scientific poster (using `scientific_poster.md`).
