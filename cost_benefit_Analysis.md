# Cost-Benefit Analysis

This document evaluates the economic feasibility of the BioSignal device compared to traditional cardiac monitoring methods.

## 1. Cost of Traditional Monitoring (The Baseline)
- **Holter Monitor:** A 24-hour Holter monitor test in Iran costs approximately 2,000,000 to 3,000,000 IRR per session.
- **Hospitalization:** A single night in a cardiac care unit (CCU) can cost tens of millions of IRR.
- **Stroke Treatment:** The long-term cost of treating a stroke (rehabilitation, medication, lost productivity) is astronomical.

## 2. Cost of BioSignal (Our Solution)
- **Hardware:** ~$33 (Approx. 20,000,000 IRR) for the reusable core + initial patches.
- **Subscription:** ~$5 (Approx. 3,000,000 IRR) per month for cloud analytics and doctor access.
- **Total First-Year Cost for Patient:** ~$93 (Approx. 55,000,000 IRR).

## 3. The "Benefit" (Return on Investment)
- **Prevention Saves Money:** By detecting an arrhythmia early, the patient avoids a potential stroke or heart attack. The cost of prevention (using BioSignal for a year) is a fraction of the cost of treating a stroke.
- **Reduced Hospital Burden:** Continuous home monitoring frees up hospital beds for critical patients, increasing the hospital's capacity to generate revenue from other procedures.

## 4. Conclusion
BioSignal is not an expense; it is an investment. Every dollar spent on early detection saves the healthcare system multiple dollars in long-term critical care costs.
