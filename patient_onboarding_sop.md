# Patient Onboarding Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

This document outlines the step-by-step procedure for enrolling patients and applying the BioSignal device during the pilot clinical trial.

## 1. Pre-Screening
- **Action:** Review the patient's medical history to ensure they meet the inclusion criteria (e.g., history of arrhythmia, age 18-75) and do not meet the exclusion criteria (e.g., severe skin allergies, pacemaker).
- **Documentation:** Fill out the "Patient Eligibility Form".

## 2. Informed Consent
- **Action:** Explain the purpose of the study, the device's function, and the data privacy policy to the patient.
- **Documentation:** Obtain the patient's signature on the "Informed Consent Form" (ICF).

## 3. Skin Preparation & Device Application
- **Action:** 
  1. Clean the patient's left chest area with an alcohol wipe and let it dry completely.
  2. Remove the protective film from the BioSignal patch.
  3. Apply the patch firmly, ensuring good contact with the skin.
  4. Check the LED indicator (should turn Green).

## 4. App Pairing & Calibration
- **Action:**
  1. Open the BioSignal app on the patient's smartphone.
  2. Scan for the device via BLE and pair.
  3. Record the patient's baseline heart rate for 5 minutes while resting.

## 5. Daily Monitoring & Logging
- **Action:** Instruct the patient to wear the device continuously for 7 days.
- **Documentation:** The patient must log any symptoms (e.g., palpitations, dizziness) and daily activities in the app.

## 6. Device Removal & Data Retrieval
- **Action:** After 7 days, remove the patch gently. Clean the reusable core with an alcohol wipe.
- **Data Upload:** Connect the device to the charging dock to sync the encrypted data to the secure hospital server.
