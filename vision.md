# Project Vision (2027 - 2035)

Our vision is to revolutionize cardiac care by making continuous heart monitoring accessible, affordable, and private for everyone.

## 1. Short-Term Vision (2027 - 2028)
- **Goal:** Launch the first prototype and conduct clinical trials.
- **Impact:** Prove the feasibility of combining Compressive Sensing and TinyML for wearable ECG.
- **Milestone:** Win the Khwarizmi International Award.

## 2. Mid-Term Vision (2029 - 2031)
- **Goal:** Commercialize the device in Iran and the Middle East.
- **Impact:** Reduce hospital readmissions by providing continuous home monitoring.
- **Milestone:** Obtain regulatory approval (IRC/FDA).

## 3. Long-Term Vision (2032 - 2035)
- **Goal:** Expand the platform to detect other diseases (e.g., early cancer detection using biosensors).
- **Impact:** Save millions of lives globally by shifting from reactive medicine to predictive medicine.
- **Milestone:** Become a leading MedTech company in the region.
