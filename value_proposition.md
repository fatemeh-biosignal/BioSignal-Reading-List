# Value Proposition (ارزش پیشنهادی)

This document clearly defines why patients and healthcare providers should choose BioSignal over existing solutions.

## 1. For Patients
- **Peace of Mind:** Continuous monitoring means you don't have to worry about missing a silent heart event.
- **Comfort:** The ultra-thin patch is comfortable enough to wear during sleep or exercise, unlike bulky Holter monitors.
- **Privacy:** Your data stays on your device. You don't have to worry about your personal health data being sold or leaked online.

## 2. For Doctors & Hospitals
- **Higher Diagnostic Yield:** Because the device monitors for days (not just 10 seconds), doctors are much more likely to catch intermittent arrhythmias.
- **Time-Saving:** The AI pre-screens the data and only alerts the doctor when an actual anomaly is detected, reducing alarm fatigue.
- **Cost-Effective:** By preventing strokes through early detection, hospitals save millions in long-term patient care costs.

## 3. For the Healthcare System (Iran)
- **Import Substitution:** A locally designed and manufactured device reduces reliance on expensive imported medical equipment.
- **Scalability:** The low cost (~$33) allows the Ministry of Health to distribute these devices to rural and underserved areas.
